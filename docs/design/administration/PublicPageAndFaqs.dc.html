Frequently asked questions

Can I bring my own cat carrier?

Yes — we're happy for your cat to travel in a carrier you're familiar with. We disinfect our own between uses too.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Call the branch directly for same-day urgent care; online booking covers routine visits.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, card in person, and bank transfer. Online orders can be paid on delivery.
