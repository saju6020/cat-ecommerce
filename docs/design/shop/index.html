Shop

Five rows, twenty-eight artboards — the storefront, the customer portal pieces and the catalogue back office. No cart, checkout or payment: those belong to the Orders area, and there is no payment gateway in v1. No live interaction; every state is its own artboard or a labelled panel beside it.
