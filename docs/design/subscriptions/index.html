Subscriptions

Five rows, twenty-two artboards, built against HO-SUB-002. Fifteen customer screens and seven staff screens. There is no live interaction, so every state has its own artboard or sits beside the others in one. There is no payment step anywhere: each delivery creates an order that is paid by cash on delivery, bank transfer or at the counter.
