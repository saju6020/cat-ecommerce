The deposit ledger counts every Accepted or PendingApproval payment whose earmark is (BoardingStay, stayId). Nothing checks that the payment's customer or branch matches the stay's, and Billing does not check the earmark SourceId at all. Exploit: a Reception user records a cash or transfer payment for customer A, below the second-approver threshold, earmarked to customer B's stay id. The worker then confirms B's stay with A's money. EarmarkMatching then stops A's payment from being allocated to A's own invoices until someone releases the earmark. This is an insider or mistaken-entry path, and cash-up should catch it. Fix: in GetManyAsync, also filter `p.CustomerId == stay.CustomerId` (join boarding.stay) and `p.BranchId == stay.BranchId`. Alternatively, have Billing check a BoardingStay earmark through a Boarding reader when the payment is recorded: the stay must exist, belong to the same customer, and be in Reserved or Confirmed.
