# RFQ & Quotation Flow

## Status: Not Started

## What it does
Procurement team generates an RFQ from an approved PR, invites multiple vendors, sets a submission deadline, receives vendor quotations through the vendor portal, and auto-generates a comparative statement for evaluation. SRS §3.3 (FR-RFQ-001..005).
